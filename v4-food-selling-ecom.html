Meet Aaji (Grandmother)

Aaji's Aawaz is a tribute to my grandmother's 50-year-old recipes. Every jar is hand-ground, every ghee is hand-churned, and every snack is fried in small batches just like she used to in her kitchen in Pune.

We don't use preservatives, artificial colors, or cold-storage frozen ingredients. We cook fresh after you order.
